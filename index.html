01. About Me

A bit about who I am

I'm a Data Engineer with 9+ years of experience taking unruly, large-scale data and shaping it into systems people can actually rely on. I've designed and shipped AI-driven data platforms across healthcare, financial services, and retail — processing everything from EHR and claims data to real-time financial transactions, POS feeds, and ML training datasets.

My toolkit is centered on Python, SQL, and Apache Spark, deployed on AWS (S3, Glue, Lambda, EMR, Redshift, SageMaker) and Azure. I build ML-ready data pipelines and feature stores, and have delivered HIPAA-compliant platforms at Mercy Health and real-time Kafka streams powering fraud detection at Prudential — while optimizing workflows that cut processing time by over 30%.

Beyond the code, I care deeply about data quality, governance, and reliability — because a pipeline is only as valuable as the trust people place in it. I enjoy mentoring junior engineers, partnering with data scientists on feature engineering and MLOps, and translating ambiguous business requirements into scalable data solutions.
